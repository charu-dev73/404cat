✦ THE ORIGIN STORY ✦

░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░

It was a perfectly normal day when someone decided the internet did not have enough cat meme documentation. They were right.

404cat was born from a simple belief: every legendary cat deserves a proper archive. A place of record. A monument. A slightly unhinged webpage with pixel fonts and neon colours.

This is not a joke. This is history. Chaotic, unhinged, completely made-up history. But history nonetheless.

The cats did not consent to being archived. We did it anyway. They would have wanted it this way. Probably.

"To document, celebrate, and dramatically over-analyse every cat meme that has ever graced this earth — with zero academic rigour and maximum unhinged energy."
— 404cat Official Mission Statement, written at 2am, unrevised

✦ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ✦

░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░
WHY DOES THIS WEBSITE EXIST?

Genuinely unclear. It started as an HTML practice project and then it became something more. Something greater. Something the world was not ready for. Here we are.

ARE THE RANKINGS SCIENTIFICALLY ACCURATE?

Absolutely not. They were written based entirely on vibes at an undisclosed hour of the night. The methodology table on the rankings page is also made up. Do not cite this in your thesis.

CAN I SUBMIT A CAT FOR THE ARCHIVE?

The archive is currently closed to new submissions because the cats are in a meeting. Nobody knows what they are discussing. The meeting has been ongoing since 2019. Updates pending.

IS GRUMPY CAT ACTUALLY GRUMPY?

Her name was Tardar Sauce and she had feline dwarfism which gave her that expression. In reality she was reportedly very sweet. The grumpy was a lie. The memes were real. We mourn accordingly.

WHO IS THE TARGET AUDIENCE FOR THIS PAGE?

People who are chronically online. People who remember when Nyan Cat was new. People who have at some point sent a cat meme instead of expressing their emotions verbally. You know who you are.

WILL THERE BE MORE CATS ADDED?

The cats will decide. We do not make that call. We wait. We document. We pixel-font everything. That is our role here.

✦ FUN FACTS ABOUT 404CAT ✦

░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░

THINGS YOU DID NOT NEED TO KNOW:

  1. This website was built entirely with HTML and inline CSS. No frameworks. No libraries. Just chaos and determination.
  2. The pixel font is called VT323. It was chosen because it looks like a retro computer terminal. The cats approved it.
  3. The scanline effect overlaid on every page is a CSS trick. It is purely decorative. It makes everything feel more dramatic.
  4. The custom paw cursor was made using a data URI SVG emoji. You are welcome.
  5. Zero cats were actually consulted during the making of this archive. This is our biggest regret.
  6. The colour palette has four colours: hot pink, electric lime, neon cyan, and yellow. It is a lot. We stand by it.
  7. If you have read this far, you are one of us now. There is no going back. The cats have noted your presence.
